# 🛒 Supermarket Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes supermarket sales data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, payment methods, and monthly sales trends using Python.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify the best-performing product lines
- Compare sales across branches and cities
- Analyze customer types and gender-wise sales
- Analyze payment methods
- Study customer ratings and gross income
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Create meaningful data visualizations

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains **1,000 supermarket transactions** and **17 columns**.

Key columns include:

- Invoice ID
- Branch
- City
- Customer type
- Gender
- Product line
- Unit price
- Quantity
- Sales
- Date
- Payment
- Gross income
- Rating

## 🏆 Key Insights

- **Food and beverages** was the best-selling product line.
- **Giza** was the best-performing branch.
- **Member** customers generated higher total sales.
- **January** recorded the highest monthly sales.
- **Cash** generated the highest total sales among payment methods.
- **Electronic accessories** had the highest quantity sold.
- **Food and beverages** generated the highest gross income.
- **Food and beverages** received the highest average rating.

## 🎯 Conclusion

This analysis provides useful insights into supermarket sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, payment preferences, and monthly sales trends.

These insights can help businesses identify high-performing products, branches, and customer segments and make better data-driven decisions.
